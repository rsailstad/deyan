# Deyan

```
swimmer
noun | swim·mer | \ ˈswi-mər \
1 : a person or animal that swims
```

Sitting at the intersection of **cinematic hip-hop**, **dark experimental funk**, and atmospheric worldbuilding, Bucharest-based 16-year-old artist **Deyan** blends ambitious self-production with introspective, razor-sharp songwriting to carve out an uncompromising identity in underground hip-hop.

Influenced by boundary-pushing sonics and internet culture, his music explores the psychological weight of modern culture, ego, desire, and decay through self-produced soundscapes and narrative concepts.

Raised between a small town in Western Romania and later Bucharest, Deyan immersed himself in American hip-hop storytelling from age 12, launching his official releases in 2022 alongside close collaborator **stvtterz**.

Following his debut release *The World Before My Eyes (Director's Cut)*, the official rollout for his sophomore project **Seven Seas You Should Swim** (SSYSS) launched with lead singles *"50/50 Life"*, *"Jaguar"*, *"Lemon Tree"*, and the dark breakout single *"Wave: 31st Sedative"*.

---

## Sonic DNA

- **Aesthetic:** Dark cinematic hip-hop, distorted basslines, tape warble, cryptic underground storytelling.
- **Current Era:** *Seven Seas You Should Swim* (SSYSS)
- **Key Releases:** *Wave: 31st Sedative*, *Lemon Tree*, *Jaguar*, *The World Before My Eyes (Director's Cut)*.
- **Production & Mix:** Self-produced & co-engineered in Bucharest.
