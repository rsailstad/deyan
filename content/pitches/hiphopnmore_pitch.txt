DESCRIPTION & ARTIST BIO:
Deyan is a 16-year-old independent hip-hop artist and self-producer based in Bucharest. Combining razor-sharp introspective songwriting with dark, cinematic self-produced beats, Deyan's sophomore project 'Seven Seas You Should Swim' (SSYSS) explores modern culture, ego, desire, and decay through narrative concept arcs.

"Lemon Tree" and "Prophet" are the two primary focus singles, showcasing raw vocal delivery, intricate rhyme schemes, and high production value.
